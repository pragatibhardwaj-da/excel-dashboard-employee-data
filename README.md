## Excel Employee-Info Dashboard

## Project Overview
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 61,067 employee records to evaluate workforce distribution, salary levels, performance, gender distribution, and hiring trends.

##  Dataset
The dataset used in this project was synthetically generated for learning and demonstration purposes. 

##  Tools 
Microsoft Excel | Pivot Tables | Slicers | Charts | Data Cleaning

## Key KPIs
Total Employees: 61,067
Average Salary: $55,029
Average Performance Score: 3.00 / 5

##  Key Insights
IT (15,322) and Sales (15,318) have the largest employee
populations, together representing about half of the workforce.

Average salaries are very consistent across departments, ranging
from approximately $54.9K to $55.1K.

HR has the highest average performance score at 3.03, while
IT has the lowest at 2.97; overall departmental differences
are small.

Hiring remained relatively stable from 2010--2022, generally
around 4.3K--4.6K hires per year.

The gender distribution includes 20,290 male and 20,237
female employees, with additional records classified as Other or
Unknown.


## Business Relevance
The dashboard provides a concise view of workforce size, salary,
performance, and hiring patterns that can support headcount reporting,
workforce cost analysis, and business planning.
